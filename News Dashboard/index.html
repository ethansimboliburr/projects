About Our Website

Hundreds of millions of uninformed individuals every day don't know where to get their day-to-day news. They spend hours searching different websites and sources, not knowing what's real and what's not. Our product retrieves data in real time from various APIs such as weather, crypto, stock markets, and more.


The user can filter these data sets to individualize their experience based on the information they are particularly interested in. We focus on a modern, clean, and straightforward interface to make your news consumption effortless.

How It Works

  • Data is pulled in real-time from multiple trusted sources via APIs
  • Users can select which categories they are interested in (e.g., crypto, weather, stocks)
  • The dashboard displays only the most relevant information in a clean, uncluttered layout
  • Filters and settings allow you to personalize your news feed easily

User Guide

Navigation & Features

  • News — Browse live headlines across seven categories: General, Business, Technology, Entertainment, Sports, Science, and Health. Click any category button at the top to toggle that feed on or off. Multiple categories can be active at the same time.
  • Stocks — View real-time quotes for a curated set of tracked symbols. Each card shows the current price and daily change. Use the dropdown to look up any additional ticker not shown by default.
  • Weather — Select a city from the dropdown menu to instantly load live conditions including temperature, humidity, wind speed, and a general forecast description. Switch cities at any time to compare conditions.
  • Sports — Get the latest news for your favourite leagues. Click the league buttons — NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, MLS, or Premier League — to expand or collapse that league's news feed. Multiple leagues can be open simultaneously.
  • Navigating between pages — Use the navigation bar at the top of any page, or click the shortcut cards on this home page, to jump between sections instantly.
